**# Week 1 – Project Scoping and Data Inventory**



**## 1. Project Overview**



**\*\*Role:\*\* Data Analyst Intern**  

**\*\*Domain:\*\* Healthcare Analytics**  

**\*\*Project Area:\*\* Patient Journey and Operational Analysis**



**The objective of this project is to understand how data analytics can help evaluate patient journeys, identify operational challenges and support evidence-based decisions.**



**## 2. Business Problem**



**Healthcare organisations need to understand how patients move through different stages of their service journey.**



**This project explores a general seven-stage patient journey:**



**1. Awareness**

**2. Enquiry**

**3. Consultation**

**4. Diagnostics**

**5. Booking**

**6. Service Delivery**

**7. Follow-up**



**## 3. Analytical Questions**



**The analysis is guided by the following questions:**



**1. How can patient progression across service stages be measured?**

**2. How can drop-off points be identified?**

**3. What factors may be associated with missed appointments?**

**4. How can waiting times and satisfaction be analysed?**

**5. How can conversion improvements be evaluated through scenario-based analysis?**



**## 4. Data Categories Explored**



**The initial work involved understanding three categories of healthcare data:**



**- Appointment data: scheduling, waiting times and attendance.**

**- Patient feedback data: ratings, satisfaction and recommendations.**

**- Operational data: capacity, utilisation and service delivery.**



**No original company datasets or confidential records are included in this public documentation.**



**## 5. Data Quality Approach**



**The data analysis workflow includes:**



**- Checking dataset dimensions and column types.**

**- Identifying missing values.**

**- Checking duplicate records.**

**- Validating numerical values and business rules.**

**- Documenting data-quality limitations.**

**- Preparing suitable data for further analysis.**



**## 6. Project Limitations**



**A complete patient journey analysis requires reliable data connecting the different stages.**



**Metrics should only be calculated when the required fields and relationships are available. Missing information must be documented rather than estimated as actual business performance.**



**## 7. Week 1 Outcome**



**The initial project scope, analytical questions, data categories and data-quality approach have been documented.**



**The next phase focuses on detailed data understanding, cleaning, validation and baseline metric development.**
